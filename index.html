My Mimi,

This isn’t for an anniversary or a birthday. It’s just for you. Because I don’t want to wait for a special day to remind you how much you mean to me.

Since 22 July 2023, we’ve been collecting these little pieces of a life together. Looking through them made me smile, but it also made me realize something: my favorite part of every picture is that we’re in it together.

I love the beautiful photos. I love the ridiculous ones. And I love all the moments we never thought to record, the ones that are just ours.

So here’s a tiny corner of the internet for our little universe. A place to come back to when you want a smile, a memory, or a reminder that somewhere, Yuki is very, very happy that you exist.

There are so many more photos to take, so many more ordinary days to make special, and so much more of us to look forward to.

I’d choose you again, Mimi.
In every version of this story.

Always your Yuki ♡
